## Reading (preliminary, 4 h of a quiet evening)

- Six of eight hosts were nearly idle (others mean 0.2–4 cores), so every setting scores
  eff ~100 % and the contention column is a constant floor cost (we sit at min 2 while others
  fill the machine), independent of the setting. These traces do not discriminate yet.
- Only hostD (others mean 12.3) and hostB (others mean 13.1, max 79.8) discriminate.
  On both, contention grows with the interval (hostD 0.06 % at 5 s -> 2.76 % at 300 s;
  hostB 1.94 % -> 3.42 %) while quota changes per hour fall (hostD 106/h at 5 s -> 7/h at 30 s).
  30 s is the knee: hostD 0.18 % contention at 7 changes/h, hostB 2.2 % at 3 changes/h.
- margin 2 vs 4 changes little; margin 8 costs 1.5 % eff on the 20-core hosts (max 10, so
  margin 8 leaves 2 of 10 unused) and buys nothing on hostB.
- up_steps 1 vs 2 vs 3: only hostD/hostB differ; 2 halves the change rate of 1 at equal
  contention. Keep 2.

  the daytime hours with real contention are the ones that matter.
